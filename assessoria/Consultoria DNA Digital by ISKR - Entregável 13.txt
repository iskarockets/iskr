Purpose & context ISKR is a Brazilian digital marketing consultancy founded by Fernando Carvalho and Gustavo Teles, positioned as "Criadores de Autonomia Digital" (Creators of Digital Autonomy). The company distinguishes itself from traditional agencies by focusing on creating client independence rather than dependency, targeting established Brazilian entrepreneurs aged 40-60 with companies earning R$4.8M-30M annually who need to transition from traditional business models to digital autonomy. The core mission addresses a structural imbalance in the Brazilian digital marketing landscape where successful traditional entrepreneurs find themselves dependent on inadequate digital service providers. ISKR's approach combines corporate methodology with digital expertise, leveraging the founders' SAP and Toyota backgrounds to serve clients who value process over improvisation and seek systematic, measurable growth. Key principles include "Competência se transfere" (Competence transfers), "Processo vence improviso" (Process beats improvisation), and "Like tem que dar ROI" (Likes must generate ROI). The company operates through a planned exit strategy - providing both strategy and execution until clients can build internal teams and achieve independence. Current state ISKR is actively scaling through multiple revenue streams with an ambitious growth plan targeting R$16.5 million annually. The primary pillars include DNA Digital consulting services, strategic partnerships leveraging entrepreneur databases, digital education launches, and healthcare sector partnerships. The company is currently developing comprehensive frameworks, creating commercial proposals for various clients across industries (medical, real estate, luxury automotive, travel management), and building systematic approaches to client acquisition and service delivery. Recent focus areas include developing the proprietary Framework DNA Digital with PACE methodology, creating whitelabel CRM solutions, and establishing partnerships that leverage existing entrepreneur networks. The team is actively working on scaling operations while maintaining the core principle of transferring competence rather than creating dependency. Key learnings & principles The Framework DNA Digital operates through four sequential phases: Liberation (0-3 months), Connection (3-6 months), Qualification (6-12 months), and Independence (12+ months). This framework uniquely unifies consultancy, mentorship, and education simultaneously, distinguishing ISKR from competitors who typically focus on only one aspect. The PACE methodology (Posicionamento, Aquisição, Canais, Execução) provides 21 specific deliverables across four pillars, supported by the Bow Tie funnel system and 5S Matrix for customer behavior analysis. The approach emphasizes that 80% of B2B decisions are predetermined based on existing relationships, requiring agencies to be "remembered first" rather than competing solely on price. Core operational insights include the importance of organic content as the foundation for paid campaigns, the need for systematic processes over improvisation, and the value of corporate methodology applied to digital contexts. The company has learned that traditional entrepreneurs respond better to structured, measurable approaches that connect to their existing business experience. Approach & patterns ISKR follows a systematic proposal development process, creating comprehensive commercial documents that address specific client pain points identified through detailed consultation transcripts. The approach involves extensive customization of the DNA Digital framework for different industries while maintaining core methodology consistency. Client engagement typically begins with diagnostic meetings and detailed needs analysis, followed by structured proposals that emphasize competence transfer and independence achievement. The company uses iterative refinement processes for all materials, consistently requesting multiple options and variations before finalizing approaches. Service delivery is organized around clear timelines and deliverables, with regular progress meetings and data-driven performance tracking. The approach emphasizes building client internal capabilities rather than maintaining ongoing dependencies, with specific focus on team formation and hiring support to help clients achieve autonomy. Tools & resources ISKR utilizes comprehensive documentation systems including detailed client consultation transcripts, systematic framework materials, and professional proposal generation tools. The company maintains extensive project knowledge bases that inform customized service delivery across different industries and client types. Technical capabilities include advanced document creation using docx libraries for professional proposals, HTML framework development for interactive presentations, and systematic content creation processes. The team leverages corporate experience from Toyota and SAP to bring enterprise-level methodology to digital marketing contexts. The company operates through established partnerships and networks, including connections to recruitment partners for client team building, and strategic alliances that provide access to entrepreneur databases and specialized market segments.
